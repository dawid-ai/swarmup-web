02 Why a marketer built an orchestrator

Twenty years of performance marketing comes down to two moves. Find the opening nobody has tested. Then build the test before the meeting about it ends.

The second move used to need a team: someone for the landing page, someone for the tracking, someone for the integration nobody wanted to own. So I learned to build the missing parts myself. Then coding agents arrived, and one person could suddenly start ten things at once.

Starting was never the problem. I ran Claude Code and Codex side by side, and the hard part stopped being the code. It was knowing who works on what, who checks it, and what is waiting on me. I had become the slowest part of my own setup.

SwarmUp is the fix: one queue, six house rules, and a reviewer from a different provider on every change. It works well enough that it now builds itself. I answer questions and approve releases.

04 How an engagement runs

It starts with one question.

  1. A bet One growth question, and the evidence that would settle it. Written down before any work starts.
  2. The build The practical thing that tests it: a campaign, a tool, an integration, an investigation.
  3. The read What the results mean, and what is still uncertain. No report that looks better than the business feels.
  4. The handover Files, access, operating notes and known limits. You can run it without me.

Scope, price and timing are agreed before work starts.
